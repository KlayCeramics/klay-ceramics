💡 How Clay Weight is Estimated

Clay volume is calculated from the outer dimensions minus the hollow interior (based on wall thickness). Clay density is assumed at 1.9 g/cm³ (typical for earthenware / stoneware). Shrinkage factor of 12% is added (wet clay shrinks when fired). For custom shapes, switch to "Custom" mode to enter weight directly.
